Introduction

Golden Visa Calculator UAE is developed by Cosmic Production d.o.o., based in Split, Croatia. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Calculator entries and sharing

The app calculates on your device. Cosmic Production does not receive the investment amounts, salary, grades or family details you enter. On Android, sharing a summary opens your device’s share chooser. The app you choose handles the shared content under its own privacy policy.

Android and Google Play

Google Play distributes the Android app, processes payments and supplies purchase status so the app can unlock or restore Premium. Google Play Billing can send Google technical information about billing operations and errors. The developer does not receive payment-card details in the app. If you submit a rating or review, Google Play processes it and may display it publicly. You can manage reviews in Google Play. Google’s privacy policy describes how Google handles this information.

Android 1.0.2 (build 3). This version initializes the Google Mobile Ads SDK when the app starts and requests advertising identifier permissions. When its advertising services run, Google may collect and share an IP address to estimate location, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The SDK can also send Google sampled crash reports containing the exception type and stack trace. Identifier availability depends on device settings. Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosure describes this processing.

Android 1.0.3 (build 4). This version removes the advertising SDK and its advertising identifier permissions. Google Play billing and optional reviews still operate. When you share a Premium PDF, the app prepares it on your device and opens the Android share chooser.

iOS and Apple

The iOS app contains no third-party advertising SDK. Apple handles iOS distribution and in-app purchases. Apple may provide aggregated App Store Connect analytics. Apple’s privacy policy explains how Apple handles data.

Developer analytics

Cosmic Production does not run first-party analytics for this app.
